A QUICK REFRESHER

Thirteen cards.
Three good decisions.

Each of four players gets 13 cards from a standard 52-card deck. When the host deals, computers automatically fill empty seats. Friends can replace computers in the lobby before the next deal. Arrange them into these hands:

Front · 3 cardsYour weakest hand. Only high card, a pair, or three of a kind count.

Middle · 5 cardsAt least as strong as your Front.

Back · 5 cardsYour strongest hand. At least as strong as your Middle.

Poker ranking, strongest first: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair, high card. Aces are high, except A–2–3–4–5 is a five-high straight. Suits do not break ties.

Compare each row with every opponent: win +1, lose −1, tie 0. Winning all three rows against one opponent earns +3 extra (a scoop). Special 13-card hands and royalties are not enabled.

We check your arrangement before locking it. All four hands stay hidden until everyone is ready. A locked hand cannot be changed. The host can cancel a stuck round by returning to the lobby; that round awards no points.
